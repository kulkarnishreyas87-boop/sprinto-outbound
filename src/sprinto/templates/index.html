How each company is decided

1 · Read

Homepage, then up to 4 more pages (careers, trust, pricing) and public job boards. robots.txt is respected.

2 · Verify

Every fact needs a verbatim quote that code finds on the page. A second check rejects quotes that don't back the value.

3 · Gate

Consumer brands, agencies, compliance vendors and companies whose trust centre runs on a competitor tool are dropped.

4 · Qualify

The model proposes a buying moment and tier. Code can only lower the tier, and stage is never guessed.

5 · Contact

Hunter, for tier A and B only. Ranked by role for the moment; sales, marketing and HR are never picked.

6 · Draft & QA

Three variants checked in code for numbers, names, frameworks and length. A judge picks the best, and a human approves.
