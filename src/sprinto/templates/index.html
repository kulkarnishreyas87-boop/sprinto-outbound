How each company is decided

1Read

Homepage plus up to 4 pages (careers, trust, pricing) and public job boards. robots.txt is respected.

2Verify

Every fact needs a verbatim quote that code finds on the page, then a second check that the quote backs the value.

3Gate

Consumer brands, agencies, compliance vendors and competitor-run trust centres are dropped.

4Qualify

The model proposes a moment and tier. Code can only lower the tier, and stage is never guessed.

5Contact

Hunter, for tier A and B only. Sales, marketing and HR are never picked.

6Draft & QA

Three variants checked in code, a judge picks one, and a human approves it.
